Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who we are

ScrollRSI is made by KubeAI, the operator of scrollrsi.kubeai.info. For any question about this policy or your data, write to saidi@kubeai.info.

The waiting list

When you join, we store your email address and the time you joined — nothing else. We use it once, to tell you the day ScrollRSI is on Google Play. No follow-up mail, no double opt-in.

The legal basis for storing your email is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

Where your data lives

The list is kept in a private Google Sheet. The form on our site posts to our own script (a Google Apps Script), and Google processes that data on our behalf — nothing else touches it.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from any third party. It is served by GitHub Pages, whose servers log standard web requests; we do not use those logs to track anyone.

How long we keep it

Until the launch email goes out, after which the list is deleted. Write to us earlier and we remove your address right away.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it erased, by writing to saidi@kubeai.info. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in France, the CNIL.

The app

ScrollRSI, the Android app, turns what is on your screen into text and saves it as files on your phone. Nothing is sent off your phone unless you choose to share a file yourself.

  • Screen recording. When you start a capture, the app shows its own explanation, then Android’s system consent prompt, and records your screen as a video until you stop it or the capture’s time limit ends it. A recording can include anything visible on screen, including content from other apps. The text is read from the video entirely on your phone. The temporary working copy of the video is deleted when the reading finishes; by default the video is also kept next to its text in Downloads/ScrollRSI, and you can switch that off in the app’s settings.
  • Accessibility service. The app uses Android’s accessibility API to scroll the screen during a capture and to take the screenshots it reads. It does not retrieve accessibility text or window contents. Screenshots may contain visible content from other apps. For its bottom-of-page check it briefly compares small greyscale thumbnails held in memory; they are never stored or sent.
  • Floating control and notifications. The app can show a small bubble over other apps to start, pause and stop captures, and shows notifications while a capture runs and when a text is ready.

The finished texts, and any screenshots or videos kept with them, are files on your phone in Downloads/ScrollRSI. They stay until you delete them. The app uploads nothing: it has no internet permission, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics in it. If you choose to share a file with another app, that transfer is between you and that app.

This section is about the app on your phone; the waiting list above is the only personal data the website holds.
