The problem

Long threads don’t come with an export button.

1. Copying a day-long chat means select, copy, scroll, repeat — one chunk at a time.

2. Most apps can’t hand you a thread, a feed or a comment section as a file.

3. And what you don’t save can be edited, archived — or simply gone.

How it works

From thread to text file, in three steps.

1

Tap the bubble.

2

It reads on your phone.

ScrollRSI reads the recording on the device, drops the repeats and stitches the lines in order. Nothing is uploaded.
